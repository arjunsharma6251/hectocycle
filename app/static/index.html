Call battery qualification months early.

Every cell here must survive 700 charge and discharge cycles to qualify, which is months of continuous testing. Hectocycle reads only the first 100 cycles and tells you which cells you can already call, and how sure it is allowed to be. Every verdict on this site is real model output scored against ground truth from three public aging studies.
